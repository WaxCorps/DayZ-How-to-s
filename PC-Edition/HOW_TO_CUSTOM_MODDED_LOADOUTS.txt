"SIMPLE" This is a list where you can define extra items that should be attached to, inserted into, or placed inside the parent item.
Remember that item you adding items into is limited on space and weight. So be mindful of what you add in here.
You can add in as many items as you want, but if the parent item is a small backpack, then adding in 10 rifles will not work.
The parent item needs to have enough space and weight capacity to hold the items you are adding in here.

"complex"  is used for items that require detailed configuration, such as:
• 	Attachments with their own health/quantity
• 	Magazines with ammo inside
• 	Items that need nested children
• 	Items that need randomized sets
• 	Anything that needs more than just “spawn this item inside”
It is the advanced version of .
